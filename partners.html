Questions partners ask.

Who can become a partner?

Anyone, as a person or a business, with a verified Black Crown Markets account. You can apply first and open or verify your account afterwards. We link them by your email.

How is my commission worked out?

You earn a share of the spread on every trade your traders place. We take you through your rates when your application is approved.

How and when am I paid?

Your commission builds up in your partner wallet. Ask for a withdrawal whenever you like and our payments team sends it to your approved bank account or crypto wallet.

How long does approval take?

We reply within 1 working day. Your partner portal goes live once your account is verified and your withdrawal method is approved.

Can I give my traders advice?

No. Partners may not give investment advice or manage anyone's portfolio. The Partner Agreement sets out what partners can and cannot do.
