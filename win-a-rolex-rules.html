Section 3 of 14

Who can enter

18+ · South African residents only

You can enter if you are 18 or older, live in South Africa, have a Black Crown Markets account, and opt in to the competition on the Competitions page of the client portal. You don't need to deposit money into your account, or trade, to enter.

We check the country on your account when you opt in. You can't opt in if your account shows a country other than South Africa.

You can't enter if you are to confirm an employee of Black Crown Markets or JP Markets SA, or of anyone involved in running the competition, or a member of the immediate family of such a person.

Before the prize is given, we check the winner's age, residence, identity and that they kept to these rules. Identity verification is never needed to enter or to earn entries, apart from this check of the winner.

Section 5 of 14

How to enter

No deposit or trade needed

Log in to the Black Crown Markets client portal (or open an account, which needs no deposit) and opt in on the Competitions page. Opting in means you agree to these rules, and we keep a record of when you opted in and the words you agreed to.

Once you have opted in, you earn entries through the ways set out in section 6. Every way to earn there can be done without funding an account or trading. Trading gold also earns entries (section 7), but it is never needed to enter or to win.

You can enter without trading. Eligible participants can enter through approved non-trading activities without funding an account or executing a trade.

Section 7 of 14

Entries from trading

Optional · 1 entry per 0.10 lots

Every 0.10 lots of gold (XAU/USD) traded on a live Black Crown Markets Standard account, from when you opt in (or entries open, if later) until entries close, is one entry. Lots add up across your trades, over all your accounts: five trades of 0.01 lots are halfway to an entry.

  • Only trades held 60 seconds or more count. What you close within 60 seconds of opening doesn't count.
  • Only gold on live Standard accounts counts. Other markets, other account types and demo accounts don't count.
  • Trading before you opt in, or before entries open, doesn't count.
Don't trade more for entries. Do not increase trading frequency, transaction size or financial exposure to obtain competition entries or improve your chance of winning.
CFDs are high risk. CFDs are complex leveraged financial instruments and carry a significant risk of rapid financial loss. Consider your financial circumstances, experience and risk tolerance before trading.
