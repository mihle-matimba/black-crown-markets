The draw in brief.

Please read these before you enter.

Promoter
Black Crown Holdings (Pty) Ltd, trading as Black Crown Markets, an authorised financial services provider (FSP No. 54549).
Who can enter
People aged 18 or over with a verified Black Crown Markets Standard trading account.
How to enter
Opt in on the Competitions page of the Black Crown Markets client portal, then trade gold (XAU/USD) on a Standard account while the draw is open. Every 0.10 lots traded is one entry, and lots add up across trades. Trades closed within 60 seconds of opening don't count, and trading before you opt in doesn't count.
Opens and closes
Opens at 00:00 SAST on 1 November 2026. Closes at midnight SAST at the end of 31 January 2027. All times on this page are South African Standard Time (SAST).
How the winner is chosen
In a live lucky draw on 3 February 2027 at 20:00 SAST. Every entry goes into the draw, so the more entries you have, the higher your chance of winning.
The prize
A Rolex watch worth up to R250,000. The watches on this page are for illustration only, and the model awarded may differ.
How the winner is told
The winner is announced live, as they are drawn on the stream on 3 February 2027 at 20:00 SAST. We then contact them directly using the details on their Black Crown Markets account to arrange the prize.

Trading carries risk. CFDs are complex instruments and come with a high risk of losing money rapidly due to leverage. You could lose all the money you invest. Only trade with money you can afford to lose.
