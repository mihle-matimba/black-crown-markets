Who we are

Black Crown Holdings (Pty) Ltd trades as Black Crown Markets from our registered office in Century City, Cape Town. We are an authorised Financial Services Provider, licensed and regulated by the Financial Sector Conduct Authority of South Africa under FSP No. 54549.

We provide the platform our clients use to trade contracts for difference across forex, indices, commodities, stocks and crypto. The derivatives themselves are issued by JP Markets SA (Pty) Ltd, an approved Over-the-Counter Derivatives Provider, which acts as counterparty and principal to every contract you enter into.

That split matters, and we would rather be upfront about it than bury it in a footer: you get a licensed local platform, an approved ODP standing behind your trades, and a structure you can verify with the regulator yourself.
